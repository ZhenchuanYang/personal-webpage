Hi, I’m Zhenchuan!

I am a PhD student at the Institute of Space and Earth Information Science, The Chinese University of Hong Kong. My PhD programme is Earth System and GeoInformation Science.

I study how and to what extent environmental impacts affect human behavior. I am particularly interested in applying big data, including spatiotemporal geographic data, and machine learning to concrete social problems such as unequal air-pollution exposure among regions and groups.

In my free time, I enjoy playing badminton and hiking.
